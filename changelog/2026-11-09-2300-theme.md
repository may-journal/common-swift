### 2026.11.09.2300

> An app hands its colors, type scale, spacing and radii to shared views through one Theme package.

- Feat: `Theme` holds a `Palette` of color roles, a `TypeScale`, `Spacing` and `Radii`, set once with `.theme(_:)`.
- Feat: `Theme.standard` draws with system colors on iOS and the Mac until an app sets its own.
- Feat: `Color(light:dark:)` follows the appearance, and `Color(hex:)` reads a stored `#RRGGBB`.
- Docs: requirement 0005, the theme lessons and the README.
