### 2026.11.09.1845

> An app moves between screens, draws in its own tokens and says where its data stands with shared packages.

- Feat: `Router` owns a stack's path and `RoutedStack` binds a `NavigationStack` to it; `DeepLinkTable` reads links through one parser per host.
- Feat: `PendingOpen` holds a cold-launch request, and `LeaveGuard` asks before an exit drops unsaved work, even mid-save.
- Feat: `Theme` hands the app's palette, type, spacing and radii to shared views; each color builds from a 0xRRGGBB value, light and dark.
- Feat: `ScreenStates` shows loading, empty and failed screens with Try Again, a placeholder that keeps its content's state, and a status pill.
- Docs: requirements 0001 to 0006, the architecture views, the lessons for each package and the README.
