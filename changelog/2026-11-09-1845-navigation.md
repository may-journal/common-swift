### 2026.11.09.1845

> An app moves between screens, opens links and guards unsaved work with one shared Navigation package.

- Feat: `Router` owns a stack's path, and `RoutedStack` binds a `NavigationStack` to it; `show` never pushes twice.
- Feat: `DeepLinkTable` reads a link into a route with one parser per host, and `Router.open` answers opened, not found or not ours.
- Feat: `PendingOpen` holds a cold-launch request until the screen takes it once.
- Feat: `LeaveGuard` asks Save, Discard or Cancel before an exit drops unsaved work.
- Docs: requirements 0001 to 0004, the architecture views, the navigation lessons and the README.
