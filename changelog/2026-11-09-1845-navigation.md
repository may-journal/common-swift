### 2026.11.09.1845

> An app moves between screens, draws in its own tokens, says where its data stands and fits any width with shared packages.

- Feat: `Router`, `RoutedStack`, `DeepLinkTable`, `PendingOpen` and `LeaveGuard` move between screens, read links and guard unsaved work.
- Feat: `Theme` hands the app's palette, type, spacing and radii to shared views; each color builds from a 0xRRGGBB value, light and dark.
- Feat: `ScreenStates` shows loading, empty and failed screens with Try Again, a placeholder that keeps its content's state, and a status pill.
- Feat: `AdaptiveLayout` splits wide screens and pushes on narrow ones, and adds a readable column, an adaptive grid and a card.
- Docs: requirements 0001 to 0007, the architecture views, the lessons for each package and the README.
